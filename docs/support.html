ArrowHead Support

ArrowHead is an offline inspection camera for iPhone. It saves completed pictures to the “Inspection Photos” album in Apple Photos.

Getting started

  1. Allow Camera and Photos access when requested.
  2. Select a marker and output ratio.
  3. Optionally tap the camera preview to type or dictate a caption.
  4. Press the shutter. The marker and caption are permanently rendered into the saved JPEG.

If a photo does not save

Open iPhone Settings, find ArrowHead, and verify Camera and Photos permissions. Photos access must permit ArrowHead to save pictures and manage its Inspection Photos album.

Voice captions

Voice captioning also requires Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. Typing captions works without those permissions.

Contact

Email inspek@yahoo.com with the iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of the problem. Do not send private inspection photographs unless they are necessary and you are authorized to share them.
